The Federal Reserve develops supervisory stress testing models to support Dodd-Frank Act stress testing by projecting profits or losses for each participating BHC under hypothetical scenarios. The use of models necessitates that the inherent risks posed by using those models be actively managed, in part through model validation, in which models undergo effective challenge, or critical analysis of models by independent parties with the requisite knowledge and skills.
