#### Introduction

#### Key Findings and Observations

1. **Model Performance Metrics:**
   - The IRRBB Deposit Behaviour Model has consistently demonstrated strong performance over the past year. The model accurately captures the behavior of deposit customers with high precision, leading to robust forecasts of future deposit flows.
   
2. **Risk Sensitivity Analysis:**
   - We have performed extensive sensitivity analysis on key inputs such as market rates, customer demographics, and historical transaction patterns. Results indicate that the model remains highly resilient across various scenarios, providing confidence in its predictive accuracy under varying economic conditions.
   
3. **Regulatory Compliance:**
   - The model adheres closely to regulatory requirements regarding capital adequacy and stress testing provisions. There have been no significant deviations from established guidelines or regulatory standards.

4. **Model Validation Reports:**
   - Detailed validation reports confirm that the model operates within acceptable error margins. Mean Absolute Error (MAE) values were found to be below 5% across different segments of the deposit base, indicating reliable forecasting capabilities.
   
5. **Parameter Tuning:**
   - Parameter tuning exercises have shown minimal impact on model outcomes, reinforcing the stability of the current set of parameters. Any minor adjustments observed in parameter settings did not lead to substantial changes in forecasted deposit trends.

#### Quantitative Results

- **Accuracy Thresholds:**
  - The model maintains a high level of accuracy when applied to both historical and simulated data sets, with MAEs consistently meeting or exceeding the threshold of 5% for all segments of the deposit portfolio.

- **Sample Size:**
  - A sample size of at least 10 years was used to ensure the robustness of our forecasts, reflecting the long-term nature of the deposits analyzed.

- **Test Statistics:**
  - All statistical tests conducted have yielded p-values above 0.05, confirming the null hypothesis of no significant difference between actual and predicted outcomes, thereby assuring the reliability of the model predictions.

1. **Enhanced Data Integration:**
