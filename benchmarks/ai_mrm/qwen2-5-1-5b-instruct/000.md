### Validation Finding Write-Up

#### Overview
This document provides detailed validation findings for the Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB) Deposit Behaviour Model. The objective is to identify any areas of concern or discrepancies in the model’s performance relative to expected outcomes under various market scenarios.

#### Findings Summary
1. **Model Accuracy vs Expected Outcomes**
   - The model consistently underestimates default probabilities compared to historical data.
   - Default probability forecasts exhibit significant deviation from actual defaults over time periods analyzed.

2. **Risk Metrics Misalignment**
   - Sensitivity analysis indicates that key risk metrics like VaR (Value at Risk) are lower than those derived from peer models across similar industries.
   - This misalignment affects portfolio diversification strategies as it leads to insufficient capital allocation.

3. **Liquidity Stress Testing Results**
   - Liquidity stress test calculations yield higher stress values than those predicted by other banks in the same industry segment.
   - This discrepancy could compromise liquidity buffer adequacy during financial crises.

4. **Scenario Simulation Performance**
   - Scenario simulations show poor correlation between simulated economic conditions and observed real-world events.
   - Insufficient granularity in scenario construction impacts the reliability of long-term predictions.
