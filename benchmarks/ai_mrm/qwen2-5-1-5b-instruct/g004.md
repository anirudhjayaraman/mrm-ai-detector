### Historical Simulation Methodology

Our trading book, like many institutions around the world, utilizes a historical simulation approach to estimate VaR. This method involves simulating multiple market scenarios based on past data over a given period to assess potential future losses within a specified confidence level. The key components of our historical simulation process include:

1. **Historical Data Collection**: We gather daily market data spanning several years. For this analysis, we have chosen a sample size of approximately 365 days per year across ten years, ensuring a robust dataset with sufficient historical context.
   
2. **Scenario Generation**: Each day is simulated using statistical techniques to generate a large number of hypothetical outcomes under various market conditions, ranging from extreme positive deviations up to severe negative ones. These simulations help capture both optimistic and pessimistic scenarios.

3. **Risk Assessment**: By quantifying the probability of each outcome falling outside predefined loss limits, we derive the VaR. In our case, the confidence level selected is typically set at 99% or 97%, depending on regulatory requirements and our risk appetite.

4. **Model Validation**: To ensure accuracy, we periodically validate our VaR estimates against real-world market events. Over the past five years, our historical simulations have been successfully validated, demonstrating the reliability of our methodology.

### Governance Framework

To maintain oversight and accountability, our institutional board of directors plays a pivotal role in setting guidelines and monitoring the application of the VaR framework. Key governance measures include:

- **Regular Reporting**: Quarterly reports are submitted detailing VaR calculations and any significant deviations from expected outcomes.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Adherence to global standards such as Basel III, which mandate transparency and stringent reporting requirements.
- **External Audit**: Conducted annually by an independent external firm to assess compliance with internal controls and audit findings.

### Regulatory Requirements

In line with regulatory expectations, our institution adheres to the following regulatory guidelines:

- **Basel Committee Recommendations**: We comply fully with the recommendations outlined by the Basle Committee, focusing on enhanced disclosure and improved resilience metrics.
- **Prudential Standards**: Our practices align closely with prudential standards issued by international bodies such as FSA and SEC, emphasizing consistency and robustness across markets.
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring mechanisms are in place to track adherence to these standards and identify areas requiring improvement.
