### Sensitivity Analysis

In our attempt to validate the performance of the Fraud Detection Neural Network (FDNN), we have conducted a comprehensive sensitivity analysis using various inputs derived from both historical transaction data and external sources. This assessment was designed to determine how changes in specific input parameters would impact the accuracy and reliability of predictions made by the FDNN.

#### Input Parameters Overview:
1. **Transaction Amount**: We varied this parameter to see its effect on prediction outcomes.
2. **Fraud Indicator**: The presence or absence of a fraud flag in each transaction.
3. **Customer Profile Data**: Factors such as age, location, and previous purchase history were manipulated.
4. **Time Since Last Transaction**: Changes in this metric could indicate patterns that influence future behavior.

#### Results:

1. **Impact of Transaction Amount**:
   - Increasing transaction amounts led to higher false positive rates due to larger sums potentially appearing suspicious without actual fraud activity.
   - Lowered transaction amounts resulted in more accurate detections but also reduced potential revenue loss.

2. **Effectiveness of Fraud Indicators**:
   - Presence of a fraud indicator significantly improved overall accuracy, reducing false negatives while increasing false positives slightly.
   - Removing the fraud indicator caused substantial degradation in predictive capabilities, resulting in numerous missed fraudulent activities.

3. **Impact of Customer Profile Data**:
   - Significant variations in customer profiles influenced the FDNN's ability to generalize trends across different demographics effectively.
   - Higher variability within these datasets led to better adaptability, while lower variation resulted in overfitting to particular subsets, leading to poorer generalization.

4. **Variation in Time Since Last Transaction**:
   - Transactions closer to their initial time period exhibited the highest correlation with known fraud indicators, enhancing predictability.
   - Further away from the initial timeframe resulted in increased unpredictability, complicating prediction tasks.
