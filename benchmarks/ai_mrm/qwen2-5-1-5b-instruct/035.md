### Quarterly Ongoing Performance Monitoring Report

#### Introduction
This document serves as a quarterly performance monitoring report for the implementation and ongoing maintenance of the IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) model within our organization. The ECL framework is central to our financial risk management strategies, particularly in evaluating and managing the credit exposure on our balance sheet.

**Objective**: To ensure that the ECL models accurately reflect the current economic environment and operational realities, allowing for informed decision-making regarding credit provisions and provisioning levels.

---

#### Financial Metrics Overview

1. **Credit Loss Rate Analysis**
   - **Current Scenario**: Review the latest quarter's realized losses under normal operating conditions.
   - **Macro-Scenario Impact**: Evaluate how each macro-economic scenario impacts the credit loss rate using historical data and sensitivity analysis techniques.

2. **Provision Levels**
   - **Actual Provisions vs. Budgeted Provisions**: Compare actual provisioning against budgeted amounts to identify any discrepancies or variances.
   - **Forecasting Accuracy**: Assess the accuracy of both forward-looking and retrospective forecasts based on recent market trends and internal models.

3. **Model Validation**
   - **Internal Model Validation Reports**: Analyze the results from validation exercises conducted across various datasets and time frames.
   - **External Regulatory Requirements Compliance**: Ensure compliance with external regulatory standards and guidelines.

4. **Operational Efficiency**
   - **Credit Policy Adherence**: Monitor adherence to established credit policies and procedures through regular audits and evaluations.
   - **Documentation and Reporting**: Check if all necessary documentation is up-to-date, including but not limited to policy manuals, training materials, and audit logs.

---

#### Key Findings and Recommendations

1. **Current Quarter Observations**
   - In the first quarter, the realized losses were slightly higher than anticipated due to unexpected downturns in certain key sectors. This prompted adjustments to provision levels but has stabilized since then.

2. **Macro-Sensitivity Analysis**
   - During the first quarter, a moderate expansionary monetary policy was implemented globally, which increased the likelihood of positive net interest margin movements. However, this effect diminished over time, leading to a gradual decrease in credit loss rates as the economy returned to baseline growth patterns.

3. **Provision Adjustment Insights**
   - Actual provisions have remained stable around budgeted levels, indicating strong consistency between expectations and actual outcomes. The only significant deviation occurred during periods of heightened geopolitical tension, where provisions adjusted more sharply than planned.

4. **Model Validation Highlights**
   - Internal validation reports consistently show high predictive accuracy for future credit loss rates, with minor fluctuations observed due to varying economic cycles and sector-specific risks.

5. **Operational Efficiency Updates**
   - The team has improved efficiency in identifying and addressing potential risks through enhanced collaboration with IT departments for real-time data processing and analytics.

6. **Next Steps and Future Improvements**
   - Further refinement of the macro-sensitivity analysis will be prioritized to better capture the impact of global economic events on local industries.
   - Enhancements to reporting processes will streamline the documentation and presentation of findings, ensuring transparency and ease of interpretation for stakeholders.
