**Methodology**

The Wholesale Lender Loss Given Default (LGD) Model is designed to assess and predict the likelihood that a loan will default given specific workout recoveries as part of its recovery process. This comprehensive approach integrates various econometric techniques and statistical methods to derive accurate predictions under different business scenarios.

### Data Collection

1. **Primary Sources**: The dataset includes primary information on loans issued to wholesale customers from various institutions. This data includes details such as loan amount, repayment history, workout strategies adopted during recovery efforts, and relevant financial indicators.

2. **Secondary Sources**: Additional secondary sources were leveraged to enhance the accuracy of the model:
   - **Historical Loan Defaults**: Analyzed historical defaults to understand the typical patterns and risk factors associated with non-performing loans.
   - **Market Trends**: Examined market trends and economic indicators affecting wholesale lending sectors to provide context for our analysis.

3. **Data Cleaning and Transformation**
   - **Handling Missing Values**: Various methods were employed to handle missing values in the dataset, including imputation techniques where necessary.
   - **Feature Engineering**: Created new features derived from existing ones using mathematical transformations and logical reasoning.
   - **Normalization and Scaling**: Ensured all variables could be compared across different scales through standardization or normalization processes.

4. **Model Development**
   - **Selection of Models**: We initially considered several regression models—multiple linear regression, logistic regression, and decision trees—to understand their strengths and weaknesses. A Random Forest algorithm was selected due to its ability to handle complex interactions between variables and its robustness against overfitting.
   - **Hyperparameter Tuning**: Utilized grid search and randomized searches to optimize the hyperparameters of our Random Forest model, focusing on improving prediction accuracy without overfitting.
   - **Validation Techniques**: Employed cross-validation methods to ensure the reliability of our model’s performance metrics across multiple iterations, enhancing confidence in the generalizability of the results.

5. **Implementation of Workout Recoveries**
   - **Integration into Modeling Process**: The recovery strategies implemented during the workout phase were incorporated directly into the modeling framework. Variables related to the nature of workouts, success rates, and impact on future payments were carefully included in the equation to reflect realistic scenarios.
   - **Real-Time Analytics**: Automated real-time analytics were established to continuously monitor and adjust workout plans based on changing conditions, ensuring the model remains effective even as new recovery scenarios emerge.

6. **Risk Assessment**
   - **Credit Scoring**: Developed proprietary credit scoring algorithms that incorporate the outcomes of workout recoveries to gauge the overall creditworthiness of each customer within the wholesale sector.
   - **Scenario Analysis**: Conducted scenario analyses involving both optimistic and pessimistic assumptions regarding recovery outcomes to evaluate potential impacts on loan losses at different stages post-default.

7. **Post-Processing and Interpretation**
   - **Backtesting**: Backtested the model's predictive power using past data to verify its effectiveness before applying it to real-world situations.
   - **Interpretive Report**: Produced an interpretive report detailing the model’s findings, highlighting key drivers of loan loss and suggesting actionable insights for lenders aiming to manage their exposure effectively.

8. **Ethics and Compliance**
   - **Privacy Protection**: Ensured compliance with regulations around privacy and data protection while maintaining transparency in how personal data was used to create the model.
   - **Regulatory Stance**: Adhered to ethical guidelines set by regulatory bodies and industry standards throughout the project lifecycle.

This methodology ensures that the Wholesale LGD model provides a robust tool for predicting and managing loan losses, reflecting best practices in econometrics, machine learning, and strategic finance management.
