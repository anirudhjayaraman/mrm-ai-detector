### Validation Finding: Expected Credit Loss (ECL) Model with Macro-economic Scenarios

#### **Overview**
This validation is aimed at evaluating the compliance of an entity's implementation of the International Financial Reporting Standard (IFRS) No. 9 ("Expected Credit Loss" or "ECL") model within its financial reporting framework. The specific focus is on how the entity handles macro-economic scenarios to assess credit risk.

#### **Key Components Evaluated**
1. **Application of ECL Model**: Ensuring that the ECL model has been accurately applied across all relevant instruments.
2. **Consistency with Macro-economic Scenario Framework**: Verifying if the macro-economic scenario components have been incorporated correctly into the ECL model calculations.
3. **Reporting Compliance**: Confirming that the reported results from the macro-economic scenarios align with expected outcomes based on predefined methodologies.

#### **Validation Criteria**
- **Model Accuracy**: Ensure that the ECL model provides reliable projections of future losses under different economic conditions.
- **Scenario Integration**: Verify that each macro-economic scenario has been appropriately integrated into the overall ECL calculation process.
- **Report Integrity**: Confirm that all required disclosures comply with applicable accounting standards regarding ECL and macro-economic scenarios.

#### **Findings**

**1. Application of ECL Model**
   - **Evaluation**: The entity has successfully implemented the core principles of the ECL model, including impairment testing for various types of financial assets.
   - **Insight**: This includes assessing individual investments against their historical characteristics and current market data to determine potential future impairments.

**2. Consistency with Macro-economic Scenario Framework**
   - **Evaluation**: A thorough analysis revealed some discrepancies between certain macro-economic scenarios and underlying assumptions used in the ECL model.
   - **Insight**: These inconsistencies might lead to overestimation or underestimation of actual losses under adverse economic conditions. 

**3. Reporting Compliance**
   - **Evaluation**: The entity complies with the requirements specified by IAS 39, but there were instances where the reported results did not fully reflect the calculated ECLs given the macro-economic assumptions.
   - **Insight**: The non-compliance could result in misstated liabilities and equity, potentially misleading stakeholders and regulatory bodies.

#### **Remediation Actions**

1. **Review and Correct Assumptions**
   - **Action Required**: Reassess and adjust any erroneous assumptions made during the development of macro-economic scenarios.
   - **Due Date**: Immediate correction to ensure accurate modeling post-critical evaluation.

2. **Implement Corrective Impairment Calculations**
   - **Action Required**: Rectify the initial impairment assessments using revised macro-economic scenarios to recalculat the expected credit loss.
   - **Due Date**: Within one month from the date of assessment to complete corrective measures.

3. **Enhance Transparency and Disclosures**
   - **Action Required**: Modify the report disclosure to include more detailed explanations of any adjustments made to the original assumptions and calculations.
   - **Due Date**: Completion of amendments before next quarterly financial statement release.

4. **Training and Documentation**
   - **Action Required**: Conduct comprehensive training sessions for accountants and financial analysts involved in applying the ECL model and macro-economic scenarios.
   - **Due Date**: End of the first quarter following the assessment period to reinforce understanding and adherence to best practices.

#### **Conclusion**
The findings indicate areas needing attention to ensure the robust application of the IFRS 9 ECL model. By implementing the remedial actions outlined above, the entity can enhance credibility and accuracy in its financial reporting, thereby meeting and exceeding stakeholder expectations. Regular follow-ups will be necessary to confirm the successful completion of these actions.
