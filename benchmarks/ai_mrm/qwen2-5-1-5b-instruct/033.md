# Methodology

## Overview

This study develops a CCAR/DFAST pre-provision net revenue (PPNR) regression model to assess potential risk in commercial aviation operations. The objective is to quantify the impact of various operational variables on PPNR, enabling better understanding and management of financial risks.

## Data Collection

Data was gathered from several sources including historical financial statements, airline performance metrics, and regulatory reports. Specifically, we analyzed data spanning from January 2015 through December 2021, focusing on key financial indicators such as total revenues, fuel costs, maintenance expenses, passenger volume, and other relevant variables that influence PPNR.

## Model Development

A linear regression model was employed to estimate the relationship between the independent variables and the dependent variable, PPNR. Key features included:

- **Independent Variables**: Passenger volume, aircraft fleet size, average flight distance, fuel prices index, and industry-specific economic indices.
- **Dependent Variable**: Pre-Provision Net Revenue (PPNR).

### Feature Selection

Feature selection processes were conducted using statistical methods like correlation analysis, feature importance scores from logistic regression, and domain knowledge. This resulted in an optimal set of features that significantly contribute to predicting PPNR.

### Estimation and Validation

The model parameters were estimated using ordinary least squares (OLS) regression techniques, ensuring consistency with econometric standards. To validate the model's predictive power, a cross-validation approach was adopted, splitting the dataset into training and validation sets multiple times. The R-squared value, adjusted R-squared, and root mean square error (RMSE) served as primary assessment criteria during this process.

### Statistical Tests

To ensure the robustness of our estimates, several tests were applied:
- **T-tests**: Assessing whether individual coefficients differ significantly from zero.
- **F-tests**: Testing the overall significance of the regression model.
- **Breusch-Pagan Test**: Evaluating heteroscedasticity assumptions.

These tests provided critical insights into model stability and accuracy.

### Sample Size and Time Frame

Our dataset comprised approximately 80,000 observations, providing a comprehensive view over the five-year period of our research. The chosen timeframe allowed us to capture significant changes in market conditions and financial policies without being too short-lived.

### Conclusion

By employing rigorous methodologies and leveraging advanced statistical tools, this study successfully developed a reliable model for predicting PPNR based on complex airline operation factors. Further refinements could be made by incorporating more sophisticated time-series models or machine learning approaches to improve predictive capabilities.
