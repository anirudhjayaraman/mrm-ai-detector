### 1. **Model Overview**
The Wholesale LGD model utilizes a sophisticated set of variables including but not limited to historical loan-to-value ratios, industry-specific factors, borrower characteristics, market conditions, and macroeconomic indicators. These elements are integrated into a multivariate regression approach, which aims to predict the probability of default for loans across different sectors.

### 2. **Workout Recovery Mechanisms Analysis**
This section details how workouts recovered through various channels have influenced our LGD model. Key workout mechanisms include:
- **Partial Repurchase Agreements**: Where partial redemption of loans is permitted under certain conditions.
- **Loan Sales or Segregated Accounts**: When loans are sold off-market or placed in segregated accounts without full repurchase obligations.
- **Subordinated Debt**: In instances where subordinated debt is issued as part of workout arrangements.

Each mechanism provides insights that refine the model’s accuracy over time. For example, partial re-purchases can affect the likelihood of default if they indicate potential liquidity issues that could lead to further defaults.

### 3. **Performance Metrics**
The model's performance metrics were closely monitored throughout the fiscal year, focusing primarily on its ability to accurately predict the percentage of defaulted loans among those initially identified as non-defaulting. This metric is crucial for maintaining regulatory compliance and aligning credit exposure with risk assessment.

#### c. **Calibration Adjustments**
