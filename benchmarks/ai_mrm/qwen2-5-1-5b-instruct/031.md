### Bank Model Tiering and Validation Frequency Policy

In our pursuit to maintain robustness in managing Credit Value Adjustment (CVA) and Exposures at Risk (XVA), we have established a tiered approach to ensure that models meet the highest standards of accuracy and reliability. Our goal is to continuously validate these simulations to minimize risks.

**Tiering Approach:**

1. **Model Development and Initial Review (Tier 1):**
   - **Objective:** This stage involves the initial development of new models or updates to existing ones.
   - **Process:** Models undergo thorough analysis from both technical and business perspectives to identify potential vulnerabilities or discrepancies.
   - **Frequency:** Developed models go through rigorous testing and review within three months after initial release. If any issues arise during this period, they should be resolved before moving on to higher-tier reviews.

2. **Model Validation and Approval (Tier 2):**
   - **Objective:** Once validated, models undergo formal approval processes.
   - **Process:** After passing initial reviews, the models move into a more stringent evaluation process where their effectiveness against actual scenarios is assessed.
   - **Frequency:** These models must pass all necessary validations within six months of being approved. Any issues found need immediate resolution.

3. **Model Operationalization (Tier 3):**
   - **Objective:** At this stage, operationalized models are fully integrated into production environments.
   - **Process:** During this phase, models undergo continuous monitoring and adjustments based on real-world performance metrics.
   - **Frequency:** Production models are subject to routine audits every four weeks to ensure they remain effective and secure. Additionally, they receive quarterly updates and improvements if needed to adapt to changing market conditions.

4. **Regular Audits (Tier 4):**
   - **Objective:** Continuous assessment of model operations ensures ongoing vigilance and compliance with regulatory requirements.
   - **Process:** Regular assessments conducted by independent third parties provide objective evaluations of model performance and security. These audits occur annually, ensuring adherence to industry best practices and regulatory guidelines.
   - **Frequency:** All models undergo annual comprehensive audits to verify their continued relevance and integrity. This includes assessing data inputs, outputs, and computational methods to prevent biases or errors.

By adhering to this tiered approach and maintaining a high standard of validation and audit frequency, we aim to safeguard our clients’ financial interests while maintaining a competitive edge in the ever-evolving financial landscape. We commit to regular updates and enhancements based on emerging trends and technological advancements to enhance model efficacy and reduce potential losses attributed to model inaccuracies.
