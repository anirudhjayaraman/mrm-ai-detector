**Bank’s Model Risk Management Policy**

**Model Tiering and Validation Frequency: Vendor Commercial Real Estate Default Model**

1. **Introduction**
   The Bank hereby establishes this model risk management policy to ensure the rigorous oversight and maintenance of its portfolio-level model risk management practices.

2. **Tiering of Models**
   - **Core Models**: These include those developed internally or through collaboration with in-house teams that span across multiple departments such as Credit Risk Management (CRM), Investment Banking, Corporate Finance, and Operations.
   - **Vendor Models**: For models procured from third-party vendors, including those utilized within our CRM department, we adhere to stricter standards due to the inherent complexity and reliance on external expertise.

3. **Validation Criteria for Core Models**
   - **Data Accuracy**: Ensuring that all input data used in core models is accurate and up-to-date, verified against historical data where possible.
   - **Algorithm Integrity**: Assessing whether the algorithms underpinning these models have been validated independently.
   - **Relevance**: Evaluating if the models accurately reflect the current economic environment and market conditions relevant to their intended use.
   - **Risk Assessment Independence**: Confirming that there is no undue influence exerted by any single party influencing the model outputs.

4. **Frequency of Validation for Core Models**
   - **Initial Validation**: Upon deployment, all core models undergo an initial review conducted by subject matter experts and senior credit officers to validate the assumptions and methodologies.
   - **Ongoing Review**: Periodic reviews every six months thereafter should be performed to assess the relevance and accuracy of the models based on new information, technological changes, and regulatory requirements.

5. **Validation Criteria for Vendor Models**
   - **Expertise Verification**: Third-party providers must demonstrate adequate expertise in the areas they represent.
   - **Compliance**: The vendor must confirm adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards during development and operation phases.
   - **Documentation**: Detailed documentation must be provided outlining model architecture, parameters, assumptions, and calibration methods.

6. **Frequency of Validation for Vendor Models**
   - **Pre-Deployment**: A comprehensive review and approval process must occur before vendor models enter into production, ensuring that all necessary validations have been completed.
   - **Post-Deployment**: Ongoing audits should take place quarterly post-deployment to monitor performance, identify potential issues, and implement corrective measures promptly.

7. **Implementation Guidelines**
   - All stakeholders involved in the creation, implementation, and oversight of models, regardless of which category they fall into (core or vendor), must comply strictly with these policies and adhere to the established timeframes for validation activities.
   - Failure to meet validation criteria may result in the suspension or discontinuation of the model and require reevaluation by qualified personnel within the organization.

8. **Review and Update**
   Regularly review and update this policy in alignment with best practices, emerging risks, and evolving regulatory expectations to maintain its effectiveness.

**End of Model Risk Management Policy Document**

This document serves as a foundational framework for managing model risk comprehensively at the Bank, emphasizing the importance of thorough validation and timely updates to safeguard financial stability and operational integrity.
