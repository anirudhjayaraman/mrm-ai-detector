## Methodology Section for Retail Mortgage PD Scorecard Development

### Objective and Background

This methodological framework outlines the process followed during the development of a Retail Mortgage Payment Default Probability (PD) scorecard using logistic regression. The objective is to quantify default risk based on financial performance indicators to inform Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) Capital requirements.

### Data Collection

#### Sources and Types of Data

Data for this scorecard was sourced from various sources including historical loan files, credit bureau reports, financial statements, employment history, personal income tax returns, and social security contributions. Financial statement data included balance sheets, profit & loss statements, cash flow statements, and ratios such as debt-to-income ratio, liquidity measures, and leverage metrics. Employment history data comprised past job titles, years employed, length of service, and salary levels. Personal financial information covered asset values, debts, housing situation, and personal loans. Social security contribution data assessed eligibility criteria for pension benefits and other government support programs.

#### Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

The initial dataset underwent extensive cleaning procedures including:

1. **Duplicate Removal**: Identifying and removing duplicate entries.
2. **Null Value Handling**: Imputing missing values with appropriate methods (e.g., mean imputation).
3. **Outlier Detection and Treatment**: Applying statistical techniques like Z-score or IQR range-based methods to detect outliers and treating them according to specific guidelines.
4. **Data Transformation**: Standardizing financial variables to have zero means and unit variances for consistency across features.

### Feature Engineering

Feature engineering involves transforming raw data into meaningful inputs for modeling. For our Retail Mortgage PD scorecard:

- **Financial Performance Metrics**: Created composite scores for total liabilities, equity financing, net working capital, operating expenses, and current liabilities to reflect long-term solvency risks.
- **Credibility Factors**: Defined credibility factors like customer tenure, number of open lines of credit, and frequency of late payments to account for borrower behavior and repayment likelihood.
- **Demographic Information**: Included age, gender, marital status, and location details as demographic characteristics that can influence repayment capacity.

### Model Selection and Training

Based on theoretical considerations and preliminary analyses, we selected a **logistic regression** model due to its ability to handle binary outcomes efficiently and provide interpretable coefficients. Logistic regression models the probability of default given certain predictor variables through the logistic function.

### Model Training and Validation

Training was performed using historical data, focusing on accurately predicting the occurrence of defaults. Key steps included:

1. **Splitting Data**: Dividing the data into training, validation, and test sets (80/10/10 split).
2. **Parameter Estimation**: Optimizing parameters via cross-validation to ensure model robustness without overfitting.
3. **Model Evaluation**: Assessing the predictive power using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, AUC-ROC curves, and confusion matrices.

### Results and Interpretation

The final model showed an overall classification accuracy close to the benchmark level. Detailed coefficient interpretation revealed significant predictors influencing the PD such as outstanding debt, total assets, monthly income, and employment duration. These insights provided actionable insights for risk management, enabling informed decisions regarding underwriting standards and allocation of capital.

### Future Scope and Iterations

Future iterations will likely involve refining the feature set with additional predictive factors, incorporating machine learning algorithms for more sophisticated scoring, and potentially integrating external data sources to improve predictive accuracy further. Regular updates may also be necessary to adapt to regulatory changes and emerging market dynamics affecting retail mortgages.

By following these methodologies, the development of the Retail Mortgage PD scorecard provides a comprehensive approach towards quantifying default risk, ensuring compliance with IRB capital regulations while enhancing operational efficiency.
