Model Change and Re-Validation Assessment: Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB)

**Introduction**

The purpose of this document is to outline the steps required to assess the impact of a proposed change to the IRRBB Deposit Behaviour Model on the bank's interest rate risk management framework. The assessment process involves evaluating both the technical aspects of the model change and its potential impact on the overall risk profile.

**1. Identification of the Proposed Model Change**

The first step in the assessment process is to identify the specific changes being made to the IRRBB Deposit Behaviour Model. This includes understanding:

- **New input variables**: Any new or updated financial instruments that will influence the model.
- **Changes to calculation methodologies**: Modifications to the way existing inputs are handled, such as adjustments to stress scenarios, sensitivities, or correlations.
- **Output modifications**: Alterations to how the model calculates or presents results related to interest rate risks.

**2. Technical Analysis of the Model Change**

This section focuses on the technical aspects of the model change to ensure it aligns with regulatory standards and maintains the integrity of risk measurement.

### 2.1 Impact on Sensitivities

Analyze the impact of the model change on various market sensitivities (e.g., basis points per percent increase/decrease in interest rates). Ensure that the sensitivity analysis reflects current market practices without introducing undue complexity or uncertainty.

### 2.2 Stress Testing Validity

Verify whether the revised stress tests accurately represent future interest rate movements under different economic scenarios, maintaining comparability with historical stress testing frameworks.

### 2.3 Correlation Calibration

Assess if the correlation coefficients between assets have been appropriately calibrated to reflect real-world interactions among them, ensuring they remain robust against data-driven trends.

### 2.4 Parameter Reliability

Review whether all key parameters affecting the model behavior are reliably estimated from historical data, including their sources and methods used.

### 2.5 Algorithmic Integrity

Check the consistency and reliability of the algorithm employed in calculating the deposit behaviour metrics, particularly focusing on algorithms used to predict default probabilities and credit exposures.

**3. Validation Procedure**

Following identification and technical analysis, proceed to validate the model change through rigorous simulation exercises and empirical studies.

### 3.1 Simulations and Backtesting

Perform multiple backtesting simulations using historical data to replicate the previous model’s performance before and after the change. Compare outcomes to ensure that the model has not introduced unintended biases or increased risk exposure.

### 3.2 External Analyses

Engage external experts to critique the model change from a theoretical perspective, addressing any inconsistencies or deficiencies identified during validation.

### 3.3 Continuous Monitoring

Establish a mechanism for ongoing monitoring and updating of the model to adapt to changing market conditions and regulatory requirements.

**4. Documentation and Communication**

Compile comprehensive documentation detailing each phase of the assessment and validation process. Communicate findings clearly to stakeholders, emphasizing the importance of transparency and accountability throughout the model lifecycle.

**5. Conclusion**

Summarize the main findings, highlighting both positive impacts and areas requiring further refinement. Emphasize the commitment to continuous improvement and adherence to best practices in risk modeling and governance.

By systematically assessing the model change, we can ensure that any modifications do not introduce new vulnerabilities to the IRRBB Deposit Behaviour Model, thereby enhancing its effectiveness in managing interest rate risks within the banking sector.
