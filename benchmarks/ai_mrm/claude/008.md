# AML Transaction Monitoring Model — Quarterly Performance Monitoring Report

**Reporting period:** Q3 2024 (1 July – 30 September 2024)
**Model ID:** AML-TM-007
**Model tier:** Tier 1
**Prepared by:** Financial Crime Analytics
**Overall RAG status:** Amber

## 1. Summary

The transaction monitoring model continued to operate within expected parameters for most key performance indicators during Q3 2024. However, the alert-to-SAR conversion rate declined for the third consecutive quarter, and one scenario breached its amber threshold for alert volume. These trends warrant attention and are discussed below.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Q1 2024 | Q2 2024 | Q3 2024 | Threshold (Amber / Red) | Status |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|-------------------------|--------|
| Total alerts generated | 18,240 | 19,105 | 21,870 | >22,000 / >25,000 | Green |
| Alert-to-case conversion | 14.2% | 13.1% | 11.8% | <12% / <10% | Amber |
| Case-to-SAR conversion | 31.5% | 30.2% | 29.7% | <25% / <20% | Green |
| Investigation backlog (days) | 9 | 11 | 14 | >15 / >25 | Green |
| Data completeness | 99.6% | 99.5% | 99.7% | <99% / <97% | Green |

## 3. Key Observations

- **Declining alert productivity:** The alert-to-case conversion rate fell to 11.8%, breaching the amber threshold. The decline is concentrated in Scenario S-04 (rapid movement of funds), which accounted for 38% of alerts but only 19% of cases.
- **Volume growth:** Total alerts increased 14.5% quarter-on-quarter. Approximately two-thirds of this increase is attributable to growth in the customer base following the migration of the acquired SME portfolio in July.
- **Data quality:** Data completeness remains strong. A temporary feed outage on 14 August affected 0.3% of transactions, which were reprocessed within 48 hours.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

The deterioration in Scenario S-04 appears to be driven by the newly onboarded SME customers, whose legitimate business patterns—frequent, high-value transfers between related accounts—trigger the rapid movement rule. The scenario thresholds were calibrated on the legacy retail and SME population and have not been re-tuned for the acquired portfolio.

## 5. Actions

- Financial Crime Analytics to perform an above-the-line/below-the-line threshold tuning exercise for S-04, segmenting the acquired SME population (target: 30 November 2024).
- Model Risk Management to be notified of the proposed threshold changes, which are expected to constitute a material model change requiring validation.
- Continue enhanced monitoring of S-04 on a monthly basis until the tuning exercise is complete.

## 6. Conclusion

The model remains fit for purpose, but the amber status reflects emerging weaknesses in a key scenario. Timely recalibration is recommended to maintain detection effectiveness and manage investigation workload.
