# Section 5: Model Tiering and Validation Frequency

## 5.1 Purpose

This section establishes the requirements for assigning a risk tier to each model in the Bank's model inventory and sets out the minimum frequency of independent validation for each tier. Tiering ensures that model risk management resources are allocated in proportion to the level of risk a model poses to the Bank.

## 5.2 Tiering Methodology

Every model must be assigned a risk tier prior to initial use. The tier is determined by Model Risk Management (MRM) in consultation with the model owner, based on a structured assessment of the following factors:

- **Materiality:** The financial impact of the model's outputs, measured by the size of the exposures, balances, or decisions the model supports.
- **Complexity:** The degree of methodological sophistication, the number and nature of assumptions, and the extent of reliance on judgement or expert overlays.
- **Uncertainty:** The quality and availability of data, the stability of the modelled relationships, and the extent to which outputs can be verified against observed outcomes.
- **Regulatory and reputational significance:** Whether the model is used for regulatory capital, stress testing, financial reporting, or customer-facing decisions.

Each factor is scored on a scale of 1 (low) to 3 (high). The scores are combined using the weighting matrix in Appendix C to produce one of three tiers:

| Tier | Description | Typical Examples |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| Tier 1 | High risk | IRB PD/LGD models, IFRS 9 ECL, stress testing models, VaR |
| Tier 2 | Medium risk | Pricing models for vanilla products, behavioural scorecards |
| Tier 3 | Low risk | Management information tools, low-materiality forecasting models |

MRM may override the calculated tier where qualitative factors justify it. All overrides must be documented and approved by the Head of Model Risk.

## 5.3 Validation Frequency

Models must undergo full independent validation before first use and periodically thereafter according to the following schedule:

| Tier | Full Validation | Annual Review |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Tier 1 | Every 2 years | Required |
| Tier 2 | Every 3 years | Required |
| Tier 3 | Every 4 years | Not required |

The annual review is a lighter-touch assessment that considers ongoing monitoring results, changes to the model's use or environment, and the status of open findings. Where the annual review identifies material concerns, MRM may bring forward the full validation.

## 5.4 Triggers for Re-validation

Independent of the scheduled cycle, re-validation is required when:

1. A material model change is implemented, as defined in Section 7;
2. Ongoing performance monitoring breaches red thresholds for two consecutive periods;
3. The model is applied to a new product, portfolio, or purpose; or
4. A regulatory or audit finding calls the model's reliability into question.

## 5.5 Responsibilities

Model owners are responsible for notifying MRM of any change in a model's use or materiality that may affect its tier. MRM is responsible for reviewing tier assignments at least annually and reporting the tier distribution of the inventory to the Model Risk Committee.
