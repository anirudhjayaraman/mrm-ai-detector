# Executive Summary

## Purpose and Scope

This report presents the results of the independent validation of the IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) model for the Retail Unsecured portfolio. The validation was conducted by Model Risk Management in accordance with the Bank's Model Risk Management Policy and covered the PD, LGD, and EAD components, the staging framework, and the macroeconomic scenario overlay.

## Overall Assessment

**Validation outcome: Approved with conditions.**

The model is broadly fit for purpose and produces ECL estimates that are reasonable and directionally consistent with observed portfolio performance. The methodology is aligned with IFRS 9 requirements and market practice. However, the validation identified weaknesses in the forward-looking component and in the significant increase in credit risk (SICR) thresholds that require remediation.

## Key Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Owner | Target Date |
|---|---------|----------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Macroeconomic satellite models exhibit unstable coefficients when re-estimated on rolling windows | High | Credit Risk Modelling | Q2 2025 |
| 2 | SICR thresholds have not been back-tested against observed migration to Stage 3 | Medium | Credit Risk Modelling | Q3 2025 |
| 3 | Post-model adjustments are not supported by documented quantitative analysis | Medium | Finance | Q2 2025 |
| 4 | LGD cure rate assumptions rely on a limited observation window | Low | Credit Risk Modelling | Q4 2025 |

**Finding 1 (High):** The satellite models linking PD to unemployment and GDP growth show material sensitivity to the estimation window. When the development sample is shifted by four quarters, the unemployment coefficient changes by more than 40%. This instability undermines confidence in the forward-looking adjustments, particularly under the downside scenario, which carries a 30% probability weight.

**Finding 2 (Medium):** The relative PD thresholds used to trigger Stage 2 classification were set using expert judgement. The developers have not demonstrated that these thresholds effectively identify accounts that subsequently default.

**Finding 3 (Medium):** Management overlays totalling £18.4m are applied to address known model limitations. While the rationale is described qualitatively, the sizing of these adjustments is not supported by quantitative analysis.

**Finding 4 (Low):** LGD cure rates are estimated from 2019–2023 data, a period that includes pandemic-era forbearance measures that may not reflect future recovery behaviour.

## Conditions of Use

The model may continue to be used for financial reporting provided that:

1. The post-model adjustment for macroeconomic uncertainty is maintained until Finding 1 is remediated.
2. Quarterly monitoring of Stage 2 coverage ratios is reported to the Model Risk Committee.

## Next Steps

Model owners are required to submit remediation plans within 30 days. Model Risk Management will track progress through the issues management system and will perform a targeted re-validation once Findings 1 and 2 are closed.
