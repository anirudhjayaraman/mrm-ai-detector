**Finding ID:** VAL-2024-IFRS9-02
**Title:** Inadequate justification of the significant increase in credit risk (SICR) thresholds
**Severity:** Medium
**Model:** IFRS 9 ECL — Retail Secured (MDL-0219)
**Model Owner:** Head of Retail Credit Risk Modelling
**Date Raised:** 12 September 2024

**Description**

The model allocates exposures to Stage 2 when the lifetime probability of default at the reporting date exceeds the lifetime PD at origination by a relative factor of 2.5, subject to an absolute increase of at least 50 basis points. These thresholds were established by expert judgement during the initial IFRS 9 implementation in 2017 and have not been subsequently reassessed.

The validation team performed an analysis of stage transitions for the period January 2019 to June 2024. The analysis indicates that 41% of accounts that migrated to Stage 3 were classified as Stage 1 twelve months prior to default. This suggests that the current thresholds may not identify deterioration in credit quality on a sufficiently timely basis, which is inconsistent with the requirement of IFRS 9 paragraph 5.5.9 to assess significant increases in credit risk relative to initial recognition.

Furthermore, the documentation does not provide evidence that alternative thresholds were evaluated, nor does it include an assessment of the sensitivity of the ECL to the threshold calibration.

**Impact**

Delayed recognition of Stage 2 exposures may result in an understatement of the ECL allowance. The validation team estimates that a reduction of the relative threshold to 2.0 would increase the Stage 2 population by approximately 18% and the ECL allowance by approximately £6.2 million.

**Required Remediation**

1. The model owner shall perform a quantitative assessment of SICR threshold effectiveness, including an analysis of the proportion of defaults captured in Stage 2 prior to default and the rate of transfers between stages.
2. The model owner shall evaluate alternative threshold calibrations and document the rationale for the selected approach.
3. The model owner shall establish ongoing monitoring metrics for SICR effectiveness, with defined thresholds and escalation procedures.

**Target Completion Date:** 31 March 2025

**Validation Team Comment**

The model owner has accepted this finding. The remediation plan will be monitored through the Model Risk Management issues tracking process, and closure will be subject to independent review by the validation team.
