# Model Change Assessment — XVA Exposure Engine v5.3

**Change reference:** MCR-2024-118
**Model:** XVA Monte Carlo Exposure Engine (MDL-0341), Tier 1
**Change type:** Material
**Validation outcome:** Approved for production use from 2 December 2024, with one condition

## 1. Description of Change

Version 5.3 introduces three changes:

1. Replacement of the one-factor Hull–White rates model with a two-factor G2++ model for USD, EUR and GBP.
2. Increase in simulation paths from 2,000 to 5,000 and move from monthly to semi-monthly time steps for the first two years.
3. Addition of wrong-way risk for commodity-linked counterparties via a correlated default intensity.

## 2. Materiality

On the 30 September 2024 portfolio, the change increases total CVA by $14.2m (+6.8%) and FVA by $3.1m (+2.4%). The largest single-counterparty impact is +$2.9m for a utilities counterparty with long-dated commodity swaps, driven by the wrong-way risk extension. Under the policy, any change exceeding 5% of total CVA is classed as material.

## 3. Validation Testing

| Test | Result | Assessment |
|------|--------|------------|
| G2++ calibration to co-terminal swaptions (RMSE) | 0.8bp normal vol vs 2.4bp under HW1F | Pass |
| Decorrelation of 2y and 10y rates (simulated vs historical 5y avg) | 0.86 vs 0.83 | Pass |
| Monte Carlo convergence: EPE std. error at 5,000 paths | <0.5% for 94% of netting sets | Pass |
| Independent re-implementation of WWR intensity (20 netting sets) | Max diff 1.1% of CVA | Pass |
| Back-test of exposure distribution (PIT, 34 netting sets, 1y horizon) | 3 sets fail Anderson–Darling at 5% | Partial |
| Runtime (end-of-day batch) | 3h 40m vs 4h SLA | Pass with limited headroom |

## 4. Issues Identified

The three netting sets failing the PIT back-test are all cross-currency swaps against JPY, for which the rates model remains one-factor and FX volatility is flat. This predates the change and is tracked under existing Finding XVA-2023-07.

The batch runtime leaves 20 minutes of headroom against the SLA. On 3 of the 20 parallel-run days the batch exceeded 3h 55m.

## 5. Condition

Production Support to provide a runtime contingency plan (reduced path count fallback, with the impact quantified) by 31 January 2025.
