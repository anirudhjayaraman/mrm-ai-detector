The 'Significant Risk 11/7 (SR 11-7)' guidelines outline specific operational risk management expectations for banks globally. These include:

1. Identifying significant risks by assessing all categories of risk, both internal and external.
2. Developing strategies for mitigating or controlling significant risk exposure.
3. Ensuring adequate resources are allocated for managing significant risk effectively.
4. Monitoring the effectiveness of these measures over time.
5. Continuously reviewing and improving risk management approaches.

Compliance with SR 11-7 involves not only adhering to general principles but also implementing practical controls to ensure compliance. For instance, it includes maintaining accurate records of past failures and lessons learned during risk mitigation efforts.
