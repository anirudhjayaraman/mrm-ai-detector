Title: Gap Analysis of Bank's Model Risk Framework Against PRA SS1/23 for Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book (IRRBB) Deposit Behaviour Model

* Executive Summary
  This gap analysis examines the alignment between the Bank’s model risk framework and the requirements outlined in PRA SS1/23 for Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book (IRRBB) deposit behaviour models. The purpose is to identify areas of strength and weakness that may impact the effectiveness of the risk management strategy.

* Background
   The primary objective of this analysis is to assess the regulatory compliance with regards to IRRBB deposit behaviour models. The focus is on key aspects such as model validation procedures, sensitivity analysis, stress testing, and other related processes mandated by PRA SS1/23.

* Regulatory Standards
   - PRA SS1/23 outlines specific guidelines for the development and operation of deposit-related systems and models within the banking system. These standards cover various aspects including but not limited to data validation and integrity, modelling techniques, and post-mortem reviews.

1. **Model Validation**
   PRA SS1/23 emphasizes the importance of robust model validation methodologies to ensure the accuracy of forecasts. The bank must have adequate processes in place to test these models under different scenarios, both typical and extreme.

2. **Sensitivity Analysis**
   Sensitivity tests should be conducted to determine how changes in input assumptions or market conditions affect the outputs from the IRRBB models.

3. **Stress Testing**
   Stress tests serve as a critical tool for assessing a bank’s resilience to adverse economic scenarios. They require consistent evaluation across all types of deposits.

4. **Post-Mortem Reviews**
   Regular post-mortem reviews after significant events (such as crises or major economic shifts) are mandatory. These reviews help evaluate the performance of the IRRBB models in relation to actual outcomes.

* Current Implementation Status
   The Bank currently has a comprehensive framework in place covering the four main pillars of IRRBB deposit behaviour models. However, there is room for improvement particularly regarding the extent of detail provided during stress testing exercises.

* Recommendations
    - Develop more granular sensitivity analyses for individual segments of the customer base rather than relying solely on aggregated results.
    - Implement regular post-mortem reviews following significant events to refine IRRBB models based on lessons learned.
    - Enhance detailed stress testing focusing on extreme market scenarios for accurate assessment of model robustness.
    - Continue refining the current model validation process ensuring it can handle high volume transaction processing efficiently.

* Conclusion
    This gap analysis highlights several opportunities for enhancement towards better aligning with regulatory standards set forth in PRA SS1/23. Continuous review and revision of the model risk framework will contribute to improved regulatory compliance and enhanced overall risk management strategies.
