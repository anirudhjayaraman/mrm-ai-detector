2. Introduction

The primary goal of any financial institution looking to lend money to its customers or potential customers through revolving credit facilities is to accurately assess their creditworthiness before extending them funds. This assessment involves evaluating various factors such as income, expense patterns, debt obligations, payment history, credit utilization ratio, and other relevant details. With these factors considered, lenders can make informed decisions regarding loan approval and interest rates. However, there is always room for improvement and expansion when creating models that predict a borrower's likelihood of repaying loans based on past borrowing behavior.
